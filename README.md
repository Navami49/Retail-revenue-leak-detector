
# Retail Revenue Leak Detector

## About the Project
This project is about finding out where a retail business is losing money and understanding why some products make less profit than others.
I used Python to analyze the sales data, PostgreSQL to write SQL queries, and Tableau to create a dashboard. The project helped me identify loss-making products, understand discount patterns, and compare the performance of different regions.
The main goal of this project is to help a business understand its sales and profit better and identify areas that need improvement.


## Tools I Used
- **Python:** To analyze the sales data and find patterns.
- **PostgreSQL:** To store the data and answer business questions using SQL queries.
- **Tableau:** To create a dashboard showing sales, profit, and areas where the business is losing money.
- **Pandas:** To work with and analyze the dataset in Python.


## What I Found
- The business made around $2.29 million in sales and $286,398 in profit.
- Tables had the biggest loss, followed by Bookcases and Supplies.
- Some products sold at high discounts had negative average profits.
- The West region made the highest profit, while the Central region made the lowest.
- The Cubify CubeX 3D Printer Double Head Print had the biggest loss among the products I checked.

## What I Recommend
- Check why some products are losing money.
- Review high discounts and see how they affect profit.
- Find ways to improve profit in the Central region.
- Use the dashboard to keep track of sales and profit regularly.



## Dataset
I used the Sample Superstore dataset, which contains retail order details such as sales, profit, discounts, products, customers, and regions.
The dataset contains 9,994 records and 21 columns.

## What I Learned
While working on this project, I practised cleaning and analyzing data with Python, writing SQL queries in PostgreSQL, and building a dashboard in Tableau. I also learned how to use data to find business problems and suggest improvements.
